What is Mental Health?

Mental health is the state of a person's emotional and mental well-being that impacts the way someone thinks, feels, and the way they handle stress in their life. Every year we dedicate the month of May to educating people about mental health and showing support to people who are struggling with their mental health through various activities in our community.

Common feelings that you may experience:

Depression

The feeling of having a weight of the entire world on your shoulders, lack of energy to do anything, and the feeling of losing interest in the things that should bring you joy.

Anxiety

The feeling of being nervous, restless, tense, and having a sense of impending danger.

PTSD

Post-traumatic stress disorder is a mental health condition that can be triggered by experiencing or witnessing a terrifying event. Symptoms may include flashbacks, nightmares, and severe anxiety.

Fear

The feeling of being afraid or worried about something.

Overwhelming

The feeling of being swamped or unable to cope with the demands of life.
